# Initial Concept

## Product Vision
A Dark Fantasy Auto-Battler that blends deep strategic combat with dual-progression loops. Built on a robust Unity 6.3 framework, it caters to both competitive players seeking depth and developers looking for a reference architecture. The game evolves from a gritty medieval dark fantasy aesthetic into a world corrupted by high-tech "Void" or "Cyberpunk" influences.

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** Unity Developers looking for a reference framework or starting point.
- **Secondary:** Competitive PC strategy gamers (fans of TFT/Dota Underlords) who enjoy deep system mastery.

## Core Features & Differentiators
- **Dual Game Modes:**
    1.  **Roguelike Mode:** Classic run-based gameplay where players use gold to roll for equipment cards each round.
    2.  **Progression (Farming) Mode:** Players farm for permanent gear that persists between runs.
- **Hybrid Theme:** Starts as Dark Fantasy (Knights, Mages, Dragons) with future potential to introduce Cyberpunk/Void elements.
- **Equipment System:**
    -   **Card-Based Drops:** Round-based equipment cards.
    -   **Smart Auto-Equip:** Units automatically equip the best available gear based on their class/stats.
    -   **Mode-Specific Acquisition:** Gold rerolling for gear is restricted to the Roguelike mode; the Farming mode focuses on permanent loot acquisition.
- **Exciting Combat:** Visually rewarding battles with impactful VFX and sound.
- **Lottery Loot System:** Boss fights conclude with a dramatic chest drop featuring a "Slot Machine" style reward mechanism.

## Key Systems (Day 1 Focus)
- **Class Hierarchy:** Deep class trees (e.g., Void Knight, Pyromancer) without Race restrictions.
- **Void/Corruption Mechanic:** A central thematic and gameplay system influencing unit power or battlefield conditions.
- **Economy:** Robust gold management, shop tiers, and reroll logic for units (and equipment in Roguelike mode).

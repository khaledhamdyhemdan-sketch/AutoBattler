# Product Guidelines

## Visual Identity (Neon-Gothic Stylization)
- **Art Style:** Utilize a **Stylized/Hand-painted** approach for environments and unit models. Focus on vibrant colors and distinct, clear silhouettes to ensure maximum readability during high-intensity auto-battles.
- **VFX & Lighting:** Employ a **High-contrast "Neon-Gothic"** palette for abilities and effects. Use deep shadows and dark textures as a backdrop for glowing, high-tech magical effects (e.g., neon purple void magic, electric blue smites) to bridge the gap between Dark Fantasy and Cyberpunk elements.
- **Readability:** Combat clarity is paramount. Effects should have clear start and end frames, and team colors must be easily distinguishable even amidst dense VFX.

## Writing & Communication Tone
- **Atmospheric Lore:** Item descriptions, unit flavor text, and narrative segments should be **Serious & Lore-Heavy**, emphasizing the gritty nature of the dark fantasy world and the mystery of the Void.
- **Action-Oriented UI:** Interaction feedback (critical hits, loot drops, phase changes) must be **Arcade/Punchy**. Use impactful typography and direct, high-energy language (e.g., "VOID BREACH!", "LEGENDARY ACQUIRED!") to maintain excitement.

## User Interface (UI) & Interaction
- **Persistent Dashboard:** Maintain an **Information-Rich** layout where core unit stats, synergies, and equipment icons are readily visible for strategic planning.
- **Dynamic Feedback:** Use **Contextual/Dynamic** animations for systems like "Smart Auto-Equip." When gear is automatically assigned, provide clear, animated notifications on the unit and in the HUD to inform the player of the change without requiring manual inspection.
- **Responsiveness:** All UI elements should feel tactile, with subtle hover and click animations that reinforce the "physicality" of the game world.

## Audio & Soundscape
- **Impact-Driven Design:** The audio priority is **Visceral & Impactful**. Hits should sound heavy, spells should have a distinct "punch," and loot drops—especially from the boss chest lottery—should be accompanied by rewarding, high-fidelity sound cues that mimic the excitement of a slot machine win.
- **Hybrid Textures:** (Optional Expansion) Blend traditional orchestral elements with synthetic, glitchy textures to aurally represent the encroaching corruption of the world.
